    Added

    • vis/levy_inspector.py — Lévy triplet diagnostic inspector and dual-panel density visualizer: Decomposes \((b, \sigma^2, \nu)\), classifies finite activity (\(\int \nu(dx) < \infty\)) vs infinite activity (\(\nu(\mathbb{R}) = \infty\)), finite vs infinite path variation, estimates Blumenthal-Getoor index \(\beta \in [0, 2]\) and singularity order \(s\), and plots near-zero singularity zoom vs tail decay on semi-log scale.
    • vis/surface.py — 3D implied volatility surface and cross-model smile visualizer: Computes and renders 3D \((K, T, \sigma_{\mathrm{IV}})\) surfaces with isoline contours, cross-model smile comparison curves across diffusions, jumps, and stochastic volatility models, and volatility term structure plots.
    • vis/story_dag.py — Process composition DAG visualizer and multi-format exporter: Walks CompositionNode trees and exports to ASCII / Unicode tree structures, Mermaid flowcharts for Markdown previews, Graphviz DOT files, and publication-quality hierarchical NetworkX + Matplotlib diagrams.
    • vis/__init__.py — Unified visualization namespace exposing all diagnostic, surface, and DAG tools.
    • examples/visualization_example.py — End-to-end demonstration script generating sample plots and diagrams.
    • tests/unit/test_vis.py — Comprehensive unit test suite for all visualization submodules.
    • sim/milstein.py — High-order 1D and multivariate commutative Milstein simulation schemes: Order 1.0 strong convergence with analytical or automatic centered finite-difference diffusion derivatives.
    • sim/runge_kutta.py — Derivative-free Stochastic Runge-Kutta schemes: Strong order 1.0 Kloeden-Platen scheme (matching Milstein accuracy without diffusion derivatives) and weak order 2.0 scheme for expectation evaluation.
    • sim/qmc.py — Quasi-Monte Carlo simulation engine: Sobol and Halton low-discrepancy sequences, Owen scrambling, and hierarchical Brownian bridge path construction.
    • sim/variance_reduction.py — Comprehensive variance reduction toolkit: Optimal single/multivariate control variate regression estimator, antithetic Gaussian and Brownian paths, Girsanov importance sampling with Radon-Nikodym likelihood weights, and 1D stratified uniform sampling.
    • sim/predictor_corrector.py — Drift-implicit predictor-corrector schemes for stiff SDEs: Unconditionally stable trapezoidal integration for stiff mean-reverting processes.
    • sim/mlmc.py — Giles (2008) Multilevel Monte Carlo (MLMC) framework: Optimal \(O(\epsilon^{-2})\) estimation complexity, adaptive level selection, and optimal variance allocation.
    • examples/simulation_backends_example.py — Comprehensive demonstration of all advanced simulation backends.
    • zoo/beyond/cbi.py — CBISubordinator (CBIProcess, DawsonWatanabeProcess, ContinuousStateBranching): Dawson-Watanabe continuous-state branching process with continuous and jump immigration (Kawazu & Watanabe 1971; Dawson & Li 2012): \(dX_t = (a - b X_t) dt + \sqrt{c X_t} dW_t + dJ_t\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features exact closed-form Riccati solution \(v(t, \lambda)\), affine Laplace transform \(\mathbb{E}[e^{-\lambda X_t}]\), exact conditional mean and variance with compound Poisson immigration, analytical extinction probability for pure CSBP, and high-order Milstein simulation with non-negative reflection. References: Kawazu & Watanabe (1971); Dawson & Li (2012); Li (2011); Grey (1974).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_cbi Dawson-Watanabe Milstein sampler with jump immigration.
    • zoo/beyond/chen.py — ChenModel (ChenThreeFactor, ChenShortRate, ChenProcess): Chen (1996) three-factor interest rate model with stochastic central tendency (mean) and stochastic variance: \(dr_t = k (\theta_t - r_t) dt + \sqrt{\zeta_t r_t} dW_{1,t}\), \(d\theta_t = \kappa (\bar{\theta} - \theta_t) dt + \alpha \sqrt{\theta_t} dW_{2,t}\), \(d\zeta_t = \lambda (\bar{\zeta} - \zeta_t) dt + \beta \sqrt{\zeta_t} dW_{3,t}\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features exact closed-form expectations, Feller condition analytics, exact 10-dimensional matrix exponential moment system for second moments and conditional variance, term structure zero-coupon bond pricing and yield curve, and coupled 3-factor simulation. References: Chen (1996).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_chen coupled 3-factor path sampler.
    • zoo/beyond/regime_switching_gbm.py — RegimeSwitchingGBM (RSGBM, RegimeSwitchingBlackScholes, MarkovSwitchingGBM): Continuous-time Markov-modulated Black-Scholes diffusion of Hamilton (1989), Naik (1993), and Guo (2001): \(dS_t = \mu(Y_t) S_t dt + \sigma(Y_t) S_t dW_t\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features exact CTMC transition probabilities \(P(t) = \exp(Q t)\), stationary probability distribution \(\boldsymbol{\pi}\), arbitrary order exact matrix exponential moments \(\mathbb{E}[S_t^n \mid S_0, Y_0]\), continuous characteristic function via matrix exponentials, closed-form Gil-Pelaez European call and put option pricing with machine-precision put-call parity, and coupled Markov-jump and diffusion path simulation. References: Hamilton (1989); Naik (1993); Guo (2001); Elliott, Aggoun, & Moore (1994).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_regime_switching_gbm coupled Markov chain and diffusion path sampler.
    • zoo/beyond/jacobi.py — JacobiProcess (JacobiDiffusion, WrightFisherDiffusion, Jacobi): Jacobi bounded diffusion model on \([y_{\min}, y_{\max}]\) (canonical \([0, 1]\)) of Ackerer, Filipović, & Turnes (2018): \(dY_t = \kappa (\theta - Y_t) dt + \sigma \sqrt{\frac{(Y_t - y_{\min})(y_{\max} - Y_t)}{y_{\max} - y_{\min}}} dW_t\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features exact Beta stationary law \(\mathrm{Beta}(\alpha, \beta)\), exact polynomial moment propagation (mean, second moment, and conditional variance), Feller boundary classifications at both lower and upper endpoints (entrance vs regular reflecting), and high-order Milstein path simulation with strict boundary preservation. References: Ackerer, Filipović, & Turnes (2018); Karlin & Taylor (1981); Filipović & Larsson (2016).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_jacobi Milstein path simulator for bounded intervals.
    • zoo/beyond/bns.py — BNSModel (BNSProcess, BarndorffNielsenShephard, BNS): Non-Gaussian Ornstein-Uhlenbeck stochastic volatility model of Ole E. Barndorff-Nielsen & Neil Shephard (2001): \(dX_t = (\mu + \beta \sigma_t^2) dt + \sigma_t dW_t + \rho dZ_{\lambda t}, \quad d\sigma_t^2 = -\lambda \sigma_t^2 dt + dZ_{\lambda t}\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features Gamma-OU variance driven by a compound Poisson BDLP \(Z_t\) with rate \(\alpha\) and exponential jump size \(\beta_{bdlp}\), exact stationary variance distribution \(\Gamma(\alpha, \beta_{bdlp})\), exact conditional and integrated variance moments, continuous Fourier characteristic function \(\phi(u; t)\), European call/put option pricing via Gil-Pelaez inversion with exact Put-Call parity, Black-Scholes implied volatility smile computation, and coupled vectorized simulation. References: Barndorff-Nielsen & Shephard (2001); Nicolato & Venardos (2003); Schoutens (2003).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_bns coupled asset price and stochastic variance simulator.
    • zoo/beyond/schwartz_two_factor.py — SchwartzTwoFactor (Schwartz97, Schwartz2F, SchwartzTwoFactorModel): Canonical two-factor commodity pricing model of Eduardo Schwartz (1997) with mean-reverting convenience yield: \(dS_t = (r - \delta_t) S_t dt + \sigma_1 S_t dW_1^Q(t), \quad d\delta_t = \kappa (\hat{\alpha} - \delta_t) dt + \sigma_2 dW_2^Q(t)\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features exact closed-form futures pricing \(F(S_t, \delta_t, \tau)\) via Schwartz (1997) Proposition 1 eq. (12), exact conditional Gaussian moments for convenience yield \(\delta_t\) and log-spot \(X_t = \ln S_t\), exact characteristic function \(\phi(u; t)\), analytical Black-76 European call and put options with exact Put-Call parity, and joint vectorized path simulation of spot prices and convenience yields. References: Schwartz (1997); Bjerksund (1991).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_schwartz_two_factor joint spot and convenience yield simulator.
    • zoo/beyond/jcir.py — JCIRProcess (JCIR, JumpCIRProcess, JCIRModel): Jump-Extended Cox-Ingersoll-Ross short-rate model of Duffie & Garleanu (2001) and Maghsoodi (1996): \(dr_t = \kappa (\theta - r_t) dt + \sigma \sqrt{r_t} dW_t + dJ_t, \quad r_0 \ge 0\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features non-negative interest rates with compound Poisson exponential jump shocks (\(Y_i \sim \mathrm{Exp}(\mu_J)\)), exact stationary mean \(\theta_\infty = \theta + (\lambda_J \mu_J)/\kappa\) and stationary variance, exact conditional moments at any horizon, closed-form exponential-affine zero-coupon bond pricing \(P(t, T) = \exp(A(\tau) - B(\tau) r_t)\) with exact analytical Riccati integration, term structure yields, analytical instantaneous forward rates, exact conditional characteristic function, and exact simulation combining non-central \(\chi^2\) CIR transitions with compound Poisson jump increments. References: Duffie & Garleanu (2001); Maghsoodi (1996); Brigo & Mercurio (2006).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_jcir exact non-central chi-squared + compound Poisson jump simulator.
    • zoo/beyond/bessel.py — SquaredBesselProcess (BESQ, SquaredBessel) and BesselProcess (BES, Bessel): Canonical squared Bessel and Bessel diffusion processes of Feller (1951), Pitman & Yor (1982), and Revuz & Yor (1999): \(dX_t = \delta dt + 2\sqrt{X_t} dW_t, \quad R_t = \sqrt{X_t}, \quad dR_t = \frac{\delta - 1}{2 R_t} dt + dW_t\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features exact transition density and CDF via non-central \(\chi^2\) distribution \(\frac{X_t}{t} \sim \chi^2(\delta, \frac{x_0}{t})\), exact closed-form cumulants \(\kappa_1, \kappa_2, \kappa_3, \kappa_4\) to arbitrary order, exact Laplace transform \(\mathbb{E}[e^{-\lambda X_t}] = (1 + 2\lambda t)^{-\delta/2} \exp\left(-\frac{\lambda x_0}{1 + 2\lambda t}\right)\), exact continuous characteristic function \(\phi(u; t)\), Feller boundary classification at 0 (entrance for \(\delta \ge 2\), reflecting for \(0 < \delta < 2\), absorbing for \(\delta = 0\)), exact Bessel process confluent hypergeometric moments \(\mathbb{E}[R_t]\), and exact path simulation with zero time discretization error. References: Feller (1951); Pitman & Yor (1982); Revuz & Yor (1999).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_besq and sample_bessel exact path simulators using scaled non-central chi-squared increments.
    • zoo/beyond/schobel_zhu.py — SchobelZhuProcess (SchobelZhu, SchobelZhuModel, SZModel): Schöbel-Zhu (1999) stochastic volatility model: \(dS_t = (r - q) S_t dt + \sigma_t S_t dW_t^S, \quad d\sigma_t = \kappa (\theta - \sigma_t) dt + \tau dW_t^\sigma\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Gaussian volatility dynamics with non-negative instantaneous variance \(v_t = \sigma_t^2\), exact continuous characteristic function, Gil-Pelaez European Call and Put pricing satisfying exact Put-Call parity, Black-Scholes implied volatility smile inversion, and coupled vectorized path simulation. References: Schöbel & Zhu (1999); Lord & Kahl (2007).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_schobel_zhu simulator supporting coupled asset and volatility paths.
    • zoo/beyond/black_karasinski.py — BlackKarasinskiProcess (BlackKarasinski, BKProcess, BKModel): Black-Karasinski (1991) lognormal short-rate model: \(d(\ln r_t) = a(b - \ln r_t) dt + \sigma dW_t, \quad r_t = \exp(x_t) > 0\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Guarantees strictly positive interest rates across all horizons. Features exact Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Gaussian conditional moments for log-rate \(x_t = \ln(r_t)\), exact short-rate lognormal conditional and stationary moments, Monte Carlo zero-coupon bond pricing \(P(0, T)\) and yield curve \(y(0, T)\), and exact path simulation without discretization error for \(x_t\). References: Black & Karasinski (1991); Brigo & Mercurio (2006).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_black_karasinski simulator supporting exact log-rate transitions.
    • zoo/levy/gh.py — GeneralizedHyperbolicProcess (GH, GHProcess, GeneralizedHyperbolic): Generalized Hyperbolic (GH) Lévy process of Barndorff-Nielsen (1977) and Eberlein & Prause (2002): infinite-activity pure-jump Lévy process generated by the 5-parameter Generalized Hyperbolic distribution \(GH(\lambda, \alpha, \beta, \delta, \mu)\) under EXACT exactness level. Features exact characteristic exponent \(\psi(u)\) with modified Bessel \(K_\lambda\) functions, exact cumulants \(\kappa_1, \dots, \kappa_4\) derived from underlying GIG subordinator moments, Lévy triplet \((b, 0, \nu)\), Gil-Pelaez European Call and Put pricing with exact Put-Call parity, implied volatility smile inversion, subfamily classification (NIG, Hyperbolic, Hyperbolic Cosine), and vectorized simulation via exact Inverse Gaussian subordination and Gil-Pelaez CDF inversion. References: Barndorff-Nielsen (1977); Barndorff-Nielsen & Halgreen (1977); Eberlein & Prause (2002).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_gh simulator supporting exact NIG subordination and Gil-Pelaez inversion.
    • zoo/beyond/sabr.py — SABRProcess (SABRModel, SABR): SABR (Stochastic Alpha, Beta, Rho) stochastic volatility model of Hagan et al. (2002): \(dF_t = \alpha_t F_t^\beta dW_t^F, \quad d\alpha_t = \nu \alpha_t dW_t^\alpha\) with \(\mathbb{E}[dW_t^F dW_t^\alpha] = \rho dt\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features Hagan et al. (2002) asymptotic Black-76 implied volatility closed form with Obłój (2008) corrections, at-the-money (ATM) volatility formula, Black-76 European Call and Put pricing satisfying Put-Call parity, exact forward and log-normal volatility moments, smile backbone classification, and coupled vectorized simulation via log-Euler and Euler schemes. References: Hagan, Kumar, Lesniewski, & Woodward (2002); Obłój (2008).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_sabr simulator supporting coupled forward price and stochastic volatility paths.
    • zoo/beyond/hull_white.py — HullWhiteProcess (HullWhite, HW1F, ExtendedVasicek): Hull-White (1990) one-factor Gaussian short-rate model (extended Vasicek): \(dr_t = a(b - r_t) dt + \sigma dW_t\) with mean-reversion speed \(a > 0\), volatility \(\sigma > 0\), and long-term target rate \(b \in \mathbb{R}\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features exact zero-coupon bond pricing \(P(t, T) = A(t, T) \exp(-B(t, T) r_t)\), spot yield curve, instantaneous forward rates, conditional short rate and integrated rate moments, autocovariance, Jamshidian (1989) closed-form European bond Call and Put options with exact Put-Call parity, caplets and floorlets, and exact path simulation without time discretization error. References: Hull & White (1990, 1993); Jamshidian (1989); Vasicek (1977).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_hull_white simulator with exact Gaussian transitions and Euler fallback.
    • zoo/beyond/bates.py — BatesProcess (BatesModel, Bates96, SVJ): Bates (1996) stochastic volatility with jumps model coupling the Heston (1993) CIR variance diffusion with Merton (1976) log-normal price jumps: \(dS_t = (\mu - q - \lambda \bar{k}) S_t dt + \sqrt{V_t} S_t dW_t^S + S_{t-} (e^Y - 1) dN_t\), \(dV_t = \kappa (\theta - V_t) dt + \sigma_v \sqrt{V_t} dW_t^V\) with \(\mathbb{E}[dW^S dW^V] = \rho dt\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features continuous Albrecher et al. (2007) characteristic function factored with compound Poisson jumps, drift compensator \(-\lambda \bar{k}\), high-precision cumulants \(\kappa_1, \dots, \kappa_4\), European Call and Put pricing via Gil–Pelaez Fourier inversion, exact Put-Call parity, Black–Scholes implied volatility smile inversion, and vectorized path simulation via Lord et al. (2010) full-truncation CIR scheme and Poisson jump shocks. References: Bates (1996); Heston (1993); Albrecher et al. (2007); Lord et al. (2010).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_bates path simulator supporting both log-return \(X_t\) and instantaneous variance \(V_t\).
    • zoo/levy/kou.py — KouJumpDiffusion (KouProcess, Kou72, Kou02): Kou (2002) double exponential jump-diffusion process combining continuous Brownian motion with asymmetric double exponential jump shocks: \(X_t = \mu t + \sigma W_t + \sum_{i=1}^{N_t} Y_i\), \(f_Y(y) = p \eta_1 e^{-\eta_1 y}\mathbb{I}_{y \ge 0} + (1-p) \eta_2 e^{\eta_2 y}\mathbb{I}_{y < 0}\) under EXACT exactness level. Features exact closed-form Lévy triplet \((b, \sigma^2, \nu)\) with analytical truncated drift integral, characteristic exponent \(\psi(u)\), analytical cumulants of all orders \(n\), European Call and Put option pricing via Gil–Pelaez / Carr–Madan Fourier inversion, Put-Call parity, Black–Scholes implied volatility smile/skew inversion, and exact simulation with zero time discretization error. References: Kou (2002); Cont & Tankov (2004).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_kou exact path simulator with zero discretization error.
    • zoo/beyond/cev.py — CEVProcess (CEV, ConstantElasticityOfVariance): Constant Elasticity of Variance (CEV) local volatility diffusion process \(dS_t = \mu S_t dt + \sigma S_t^\gamma dW_t, S_0 = s_0 > 0\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness. Features state-dependent local return volatility \(\sigma_{\text{loc}}(S) = \sigma S^{\gamma - 1}\), constant return variance elasticity \(2(\gamma - 1)\), boundary classification with absorbing zero (default / bankruptcy risk) for \(\gamma < 1\), exact European option pricing via Schroder's (1989) non-central chi-squared representation across equity leverage (\(\gamma < 1\)), geometric Brownian motion (\(\gamma = 1\)), and commodity inverse leverage (\(\gamma > 1\)) regimes, exact Put-Call parity, analytical absorption probability \(P(S_T = 0) = 1 - F_{\chi^2}(x; 1/(1-\gamma))\), transition CDF, analytical Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Vega), conditional moments (closed-form for \(\gamma \in \{0.5, 1.0\}\) and quadrature propagation for general \(\gamma\)), and vectorized path simulation. References: Cox (1975); Schroder (1989); Emanuel & MacBeth (1982); Choi & Wu (2021).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_cev simulation routine supporting second-order Milstein and Euler–Maruyama schemes with absorbing boundary conditions at zero and exact geometric Brownian motion for \(\gamma = 1\).
    • zoo/levy/merton.py — MertonJumpDiffusion (MertonProcess, Merton76): Merton (1976) jump-diffusion process combining continuous Brownian motion with normally distributed compound Poisson jump shocks: \(X_t = \mu t + \sigma W_t + \sum_{i=1}^{N_t} Y_i\), \(Y_i \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu_J, \sigma_J^2)\) under EXACT exactness level. Features exact Lévy triplet \((b, \sigma^2, \nu)\) with analytical truncated Gaussian drift integral, closed-form characteristic exponent, analytical cumulants of all orders \(n\), Merton (1976) analytical option pricing formula via Poisson mixture of Black–Scholes values, Put-Call parity, Black-Scholes implied volatility smile/skew inversion, and exact vectorized simulation with zero time discretization error. References: Merton (1976); Cont & Tankov (2004).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_merton exact path simulator with zero discretization error.
    • zoo/beyond/heston.py — HestonProcess (Heston): Continuous-time affine stochastic volatility diffusion model \(dX_t = (\mu - \frac{1}{2}V_t)dt + \sqrt{V_t} dW_t^S\), \(dV_t = \kappa(\theta - V_t)dt + \sigma_v \sqrt{V_t} dW_t^V\) with \(d\langle W^S, W^V \rangle_t = \rho dt\) under MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness. Features exact characteristic functions via Albrecher et al. (2007) continuous formulation, CIR instantaneous and integrated variance moments, Feller condition verification, analytical cumulants, European Call and Put option pricing via Gil-Pelaez Fourier inversion, Black-Scholes implied volatility smile/skew inversion via root-finding, and Full Truncation simulation (Lord et al. 2010). References: Heston (1993); Albrecher et al. (2007); Lord et al. (2010).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_heston simulation routine supporting Lord et al. (2010) Full Truncation scheme and truncated Euler scheme.
    • zoo/beyond/ornstein_uhlenbeck.py — OrnsteinUhlenbeckProcess (OrnsteinUhlenbeck, Vasicek): Continuous-time Gaussian mean-reverting diffusion \(dX_t = \theta(\mu - X_t)dt + \sigma dW_t\) with MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features exact Gaussian transition density, exact conditional moments and cumulants, stationary distribution \(N(\mu, \sigma^2/(2\theta))\), relaxation time \(\tau = 1/\theta\), half-life \(t_{1/2} = \ln(2)/\theta\), exponential autocovariance and autocorrelation, two-time covariance, Lorentzian power spectral density \(S(\omega) = \sigma^2/(\pi(\theta^2+\omega^2))\), exact Laplace transform and characteristic function, Vasicek zero-coupon bond pricing \(P(\tau) = A(\tau)e^{-B(\tau)r_0}\), yield curves, and asymptotic long-term yield. References: Uhlenbeck & Ornstein (1930); Vasicek (1977).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_ou supporting exact Gaussian increments without discretization bias (method="exact") and Euler–Maruyama discretisation (method="euler").
    • zoo/beyond/cir.py — CIRProcess (CoxIngersollRoss): Cox–Ingersoll–Ross square-root diffusion process \(dr_t = \kappa(\theta - r_t)dt + \sigma\sqrt{r_t}dW_t\) with MOMENT_PROPAGATION exactness level. Features Feller condition evaluation (\(2\kappa\theta \ge \sigma^2\)), exact non-central chi-squared transition density and exact simulation via sample_cir, exact conditional moments and cumulants of all orders, stationary Gamma distribution properties, exponential autocovariance, exact Laplace transform and characteristic function, zero-coupon bond pricing \(P(\tau) = A(\tau)e^{-B(\tau)r_0}\) via the Riccati system, yield curves, and asymptotic long-term yield. References: Cox, Ingersoll & Ross (1985); Feller (1951).
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_cir supporting exact non-central chi-squared transition sampling (method="exact"), full-truncation Euler–Maruyama (method="truncated_euler"), and reflected Euler–Maruyama (method="reflected_euler").
    • zoo/levy/compound_poisson.py — CompoundPoisson: general compound Poisson process with homogeneous Poisson arrival counter and parameterised jump distributions (NormalJump, ExponentialJump, DoubleExponentialJump, UniformJump, DiscreteJump). EXACT exactness level with closed-form Lévy triplet (b=λ·E[Y·𝟙_{|Y|≤1}], σ²=0, ν=λ·F_Y), exact characteristic function φ(u;t) = exp(λt(φ_Y(u)-1)), exact cumulants of all orders κ_n = λ·t·E[Yⁿ], and vectorized exact Poisson thinning simulation. References: Kingman (1993); Cont & Tankov (2004) Sec. 3.4; Sato (1999) Ex. 8.4.
    • sim/exact.py — Vectorized sample_compound_poisson using np.repeat and np.bincount with fallback for non-vectorized custom samplers.
    • zoo/beyond/mmpp.py — MarkovModulatedPoissonProcess: N-state MMPP (CTMC-modulated Poisson counts). MOMENT_PROPAGATION; no Levy triplet (dependent increments). Exact event-driven simulation, finite-horizon mean/variance and cumulants of any order via block matrix exponentials (with a conditioning guard), stationary distribution and mean intensity. Reference: Fischer & Meier-Hellstern (1993), Performance Evaluation 18, 149-171.

    [1.0.0] — 2026-09-19

    Initial public release. Establishes the algebraic core, the 13-process Lévy zoo, the beyond-Lévy category, the ops/sim/analytics layers, multivariate support, and the story renderer.

    Added — Core engine

    • core/exactness.py — ExactnessLevel enum (EXACT / MOMENT_PROPAGATION / SIMULATION_ONLY) with total ordering and combine() rule: operations degrade to the minimum level of their operands
    • core/exceptions.py — SpxaError, ExactnessError, SpxaDegradationWarning
    • core/levy_measure.py — LevyMeasure hierarchy: CompoundLevyMeasure, DensityLevyMeasure, ScaledLevyMeasure
    • core/triplet.py — immutable LevyTriplet(b, σ², ν) with arithmetic (__add__, .scale()), numerical char_exp() via the Lévy–Khintchine formula, and cumulant(n) (Sato 1999, Thm 25.3)
    • core/properties.py — ProcessProperties lattice tracking stationarity, independence, martingale, finite mean/variance, tail index, self-similarity, subordinator flag, Hurst index; propagation rules for +, scaling, and @ derived from Sato (1999) Ch. 11 & 30
    • core/process.py — abstract Process base class; operator overloads +, *, @, -, unary -; each returns a new immutable _ComposedProcess or _ScaledProcess instance; .triplet, .cumulants(), .char_func(), .__story__(), .__story_latex__()
    • story/narrator.py — CompositionNode tree, narrate() plain-text derivation engine, narrate_latex() / story_latex() MathJax-ready LaTeX renderer

    Added — Process zoo (spxa.zoo.levy)

    • BrownianMotion — triplet (μ, σ², 0), exact Gaussian simulation, closed-form char_func_exact()
    • PoissonProcess — discrete Lévy measure, exact inter-arrival simulation, char_func_exact()
    • GammaProcess — subordinator, exact bernstein_function() a·log(1+u/b) (Schilling et al. 2012, Ex. 3.9), exact cumulants, Gamma simulation
    • VarianceGamma — Lévy density C/|x|·exp(Ax−B|x|), closed-form cumulants to order 4 and series above, exact char_func_exact() (Madan, Carr & Chang 1998, eq. 4), exact simulation via Gamma subordination
    • NIG — Bessel Lévy density, exact char_func_exact(), closed-form cumulants, simulation via InvGaussian subordination (Barndorff-Nielsen 1997)
    • AlphaStable — exact char_func, self-similarity index 1/α, Chambers–Mallows–Stuck simulation (Samorodnitsky & Taqqu 1994)
    • CGMY — exact char_func, closed-form cumulants finite for α < 2, series-representation simulation (Carr, Geman, Madan, Yor 2002; Rosiński 2001)
    • InverseGaussianProcess — exact Bernstein function, cumulants (2n−1)!!·μ^{2n-1}/λ^{n-1}, exact char_func, Wald simulation (Tweedie 1957)
    • MeixnerProcess — stable log-cosh characteristic function, exact cumulants to order 4, Gil-Pelaez CDF-inversion simulation (Schoutens & Teugels 1998)
    • TemperedStable — Lévy density C·x^{-1−α}·exp(−λx), exact Bernstein function, cumulants C·Γ(n−α)/λ^{n−α}, CDF-inversion simulation (Rosiński 2007)
    • NegativeBinomialProcess — finite-activity discrete subordinator, exact characteristic function, closed-form cumulants to order 4, exact NegBin sampler (Quenouille 1949)
