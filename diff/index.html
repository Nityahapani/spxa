    Differentiable SDE Engine & Hardware Acceleration (spxa.diff)

    The spxa.diff subpackage provides JAX-accelerated, hardware-vectorized, and end-to-end differentiable simulation and sensitivity engines for continuous and jump-diffusion stochastic differential equations (SDEs).

    Key Capabilities

    1. JIT Compilation & Hardware Acceleration:
    2. Compiles full simulation trajectories to native GPU/TPU/CPU machine instructions via jax.lax.scan and jax.jit.

    3. Vectorized batch parallel simulation across hundreds of thousands of Monte Carlo paths via jax.vmap.

    4. Differentiable Numerical SDE Solvers:

    5. solve_euler_maruyama: Standard Itô and Jump-Diffusion discretization.
    6. solve_milstein: Order 1.0 strong convergence with automatic spatial diffusion derivatives computed via forward-mode autodiff (jax.jvp).
    7. solve_srk: Two-stage explicit Stochastic Runge-Kutta predictor-corrector.

    8. solve_reversible_heun: Reversible trapezoidal integrator for memory-efficient backpropagation through time.

    9. Differentiable Stochastic Processes:

    10. Diffusions: DifferentiableGBM, DifferentiableOU, DifferentiableCIR, DifferentiableBESQ, DifferentiableBessel, DifferentiableCEV, DifferentiableBilinear.
    11. Stochastic Volatility: DifferentiableHeston, DifferentiableSABR, DifferentiableThreeHalves.
    12. Jump Diffusions: DifferentiableMerton, DifferentiableKou, DifferentiableBates, DifferentiableHawkes.
    13. Multi-Factor & Commodities: DifferentiableSchwartz1Factor, DifferentiableSchwartz2Factor, DifferentiableVectorOU.

    14. Fractional & Rough: simulate_diff_fbm, simulate_diff_rough_heston.

    15. Sensitivity & Greek Differentiation Engine (spxa.diff.greeks):

    16. Pathwise Automatic Differentiation: \(\nabla_{\boldsymbol{\theta}} \mathbb{E}[f(X_T)]\).
    17. Likelihood Ratio Method (LRM): Score function sensitivities for discontinuous payoffs (e.g. digital options, barrier options).
    18. Malliavin Greeks: Integration-by-parts on Wiener space via Bismut-Elworthy-Li weights for nonsmooth payoffs.

    19. Higher-Order Hessian Greeks: Second-order cross-Greeks (\(\Gamma, \text{Vanna}, \text{Volga}\)) via jax.hessian.

    20. Neural SDEs & Deep Hedging (spxa.diff.nn):

    21. DifferentiableNeuralSDE: Parameterized drift and diffusion networks.
    22. Deep Hedging Engine: Multi-period optimal hedging under transaction costs and convex risk measures (Entropic risk, Expected Shortfall, Quadratic error).
